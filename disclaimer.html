Transparency & sources

Independent by design.

A useful way to explore rally results. Not an official record or a ruling.

Who publishes this site

Northern Regions Rally is an independently maintained regional information site. It is not the official website of NRC South Africa or Motorsport South Africa (MSA), and does not speak for either organisation, event officials or championship authorities. References to the championship do not imply affiliation, approval or endorsement.

Where results come from

Round results are transcribed from official classification documents, normally final classifications, rather than live timing. Manual and AI-assisted imports are reviewed by the site administrator before publication; that review is not official certification. Transcription, interpretation and publication errors or delays remain possible.

A source may be provisional, or its finality may be unclear. Those cases are labelled on the results pages and in the source notes. A document being published here does not make it final. Revised classifications, bulletins and subsequent official decisions may supersede the version used here.

What this site calculates

Championship points, standings, stage progressions and gaps are derived on this site from the source information and recorded scoring rules. They are not official championship publications. A "Points match MSA" label means the recorded points have been checked against the cited MSA standings for those rounds; it does not certify the site or guarantee that the official record has not changed.

The stage graph's "Final" point is its classification endpoint, including the recorded penalty total. The word describes the endpoint, not confirmation that a provisional or unclear source has become final. See the season-specific sources and data notes for documents, statuses, calculation details and recorded exceptions.

Official sources always take precedence

For authoritative results, regulations, championship standings and official decisions, consult NRC South Africa (nrcsa.co.za) and Motorsport South Africa (motorsport.co.za), including the relevant event officials' publications and any amendments.

If information differs, the applicable official publication or decision takes precedence. This site must not be relied upon as evidence for a protest, appeal, eligibility determination, points claim or other sporting dispute. Direct such matters to the relevant officials through the official procedures.

Corrections & responsible use

Use this archive for convenient browsing, comparison and analysis, and verify important information against the current official documents. Completeness, accuracy and currency are not guaranteed. If you notice an error, use the site contact details and identify the round and the relevant source document. Reporting an error here does not lodge an official protest or appeal.
